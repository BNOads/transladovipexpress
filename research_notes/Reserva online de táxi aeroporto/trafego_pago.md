# Paid traffic strategies for airport taxi and transfer businesses (Translado Vip Express, Confins)

## 1. Google Ads: keywords, targeting, schedule and ad formats that fit airport taxi/transfer

### Takeaway
The core search structure is "service term + airport/city modifier" (táxi aeroporto confins, transfer confins ouro preto), with "Presence or Interest" location targeting so travelers still in their origin city see the ads before they fly. Two platform changes matter for 2025-2026: call-only ads are being retired (Feb 2026 no new ones, Feb 2027 stop serving), and WhatsApp message assets are live in Brazil for Search and Performance Max.

### Cited Findings
- Primary keyword themes recommended for airport transportation: "airport shuttle", "airport taxi", "airport transfers", "airport transportation services"; secondary: "... near me", "airport transfer booking", "private airport car service", "corporate airport transfer"; add geographic modifiers and airport codes (e.g. "JFK airport shuttle"). Source: [Sona](https://www.sona.com/blog/google-ads-for-airport-transportation-services-a-comprehensive-setup-guide)
- Same guide recommends 1:1 alignment between keyword, ad copy and landing page, airport-specific headlines, separate pages per service type, and remarketing to visitors who abandoned booking or compared providers. Source: [Sona](https://www.sona.com/blog/google-ads-for-airport-transportation-services-a-comprehensive-setup-guide)
- A large share of airport transfer bookings happen close to travel time, when urgency is highest; long-tail service/location keywords face less competition than "[city] airport shuttle". Source: [Daniel Iloh Limited](https://www.danieliloh.com/airport-transfer-marketing-strategies-that-increase-reservations-7-marketing-strategies/) (via search snippet; industry blog, not primary data)
- Case study: a limo service running targeted Google Ads for "Airport Limos" and similar services went from 10 to 65 leads per week and raised conversion rate from 4.57% to 24.59% in 9 weeks (agency-published, self-reported). Source: [AgencyPPC](https://agencyppc.com/case-study-limo-leads-increase-by-650/)
- Location option "Presence or Interest": if the query mentions a location, that location is used first; otherwise the user's physical location. Google says advertisers in Travel, Real Estate and Education that switched from "Presence" to "Presence or Interest" saw about +5% more Search conversions. Source: [Google Ads Help, advanced location options](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en)
- Google cites airports as targetable commercial hubs, e.g. an airport shuttle service targeting all major airports in a state; risk of "Presence or Interest" is wasted spend on irrelevant distant users. Source: [Google Ads Help, geographic targeting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043?hl=en); [Growth Minded Marketing](https://growthmindedmarketing.com/blog/google-ads-location-targeting/)
- Call-only ads are deprecated: announced Oct 2025, creation of new call-only ads removed Feb 2026, existing call-only ads stop receiving impressions Feb 2027; replacement is responsive search ads with call assets. Source: [Google Ads Help, transition from call ads to call assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16598240?hl=en); [Google Ads Developer Blog, Oct 2025](https://ads-developers.googleblog.com/2025/10/upgrade-your-call-only-ads-to.html)
- Message assets (click-to-WhatsApp/SMS button on Search ads) are available in Brazil (launched first in Brazil and India) and work with Search and Performance Max. Source: [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/whatsapp-google-ads-message-assets-450630); [SleekFlow](https://sleekflow.io/blog/google-click-to-whatsapp-ads)
- Message asset requirements: good policy compliance history, eligible vertical, completed advertiser verification; new advertisers may face temporary impression limits; a starter (prefilled) message is configured; headlines must not mention the messaging platform; Google recommends "Conversions" as bid goal. Help page marks the feature as beta/limited. Source: [Google Ads Help, About message assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14888522?hl=en)
- Google Local Services Ads are available in North America, parts of Europe and Australia; Brazil is not in the listed markets as of 2026. Source: [Ignite Visibility](https://ignitevisibility.com/google-local-services-ads-using/); [PPC Chief](https://ppcchief.com/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide)
- Demand context for Confins: BH Airport handled a record 13.3 million passengers in 2025, led Brazil in domestic destinations (63 routes), and set an international record in July 2025 (62,353 international pax). Source: [BH Airport press release](https://www.bh-airport.com.br/pt-BR/press-room/releases?news=bh-airport-tem-recorde-historico-de-movimentacao-internacional-de-passageiros); [Panrotas](https://www.panrotas.com.br/aviacao/aeroportos/2025/04/confins-movimenta-recorde-de-mais-de-3-milhoes-de-passageiros-no-primeiro-trimestre_216884.html); [O Tempo](https://www.otempo.com.br/economia/2025/9/23/aeroporto-de-confins-tem-recorde-historico-de-movimentacao-internacional-de-passageiros)
- May 2026 projection of 1.1 million passengers at Confins with expanded flight offer. Source: [Estado de Minas](https://www.em.com.br/turismo/2026/05/7414955-confins-maio-projeta-11-milhao-de-passageiros-e-amplia-a-oferta-de-voos.html)

### Inferences
- Suggested ad group themes for Translado Vip Express: (a) generic airport taxi ("táxi aeroporto confins", "táxi confins", "táxi confins bh", "táxi credenciado confins"); (b) route/destination transfers ("transfer confins ouro preto", "confins inhotim", "confins pampulha/savassi", "confins sete lagoas", "confins mariana/tiradentes"); (c) price-intent ("preço táxi confins bh", "quanto custa táxi confins"); (d) comparison/alternative intent ("uber confins", "uber aeroporto confins preço", "ônibus confins bh" / "conexão aeroporto"), where the angle is fixed price, credentialed, no surge; (e) English queries for international arrivals ("taxi confins airport", "belo horizonte airport transfer"). Using competitor brand names as keywords is allowed in Google Search but not in ad text if trademarked; this should be validated.
- Run a separate "Presence or Interest" campaign targeting origin cities with the most Confins routes (São Paulo, Rio, Brasília, Salvador, Curitiba, etc.) only for queries that explicitly mention Confins/BH/Ouro Preto, to capture pre-trip bookings; keep the local campaign on "Presence" around Confins/BH.
- Since call-only ads are ending, any phone campaign should migrate to RSA + call asset + message asset combination now.
- LSA is not an option in Brazil; Google Business Profile optimization (reviews, "táxi" category, photos at the credentialed stand) remains the Maps lever.
- Ad schedule: arrivals peak around bank flights; bid adjustments by hour should be derived from the client's own WhatsApp lead timestamps and the Confins arrivals board rather than generic advice.

### Gaps
- Could not verify current availability in Brazil of Google Maps promoted pins, lead form assets on Search, or Demand Gen benchmarks for transport; not researched within the tool budget.
- No public data found on CPC for "táxi aeroporto confins" or Brazilian airport taxi keywords; Keyword Planner access would be needed.
- No primary source found on "targeting travelers arriving" as a Google audience (e.g. in-market "trips to Belo Horizonte"); needs check inside Google Ads audience manager.

## 2. Measurement: WhatsApp click conversions vs real bookings, offline import, Consent Mode

### Takeaway
A WhatsApp click is a weak proxy: the stronger setup is to pass a click ID (GCLID/fbclid or a short code) into the WhatsApp conversation, mark actual bookings in a CRM/sheet, and upload them back as conversions (Google enhanced conversions for leads / Data Manager; Meta Conversions API for Business Messaging). Several deadlines hit in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Google recommends enhanced conversions for leads over legacy offline conversion import (easier setup, more durable and accurate, cross-device, engaged-view conversions). Source: [Google Ads Help, upgrade offline import to ECL](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14274408?hl=en)
- From June 15, 2026, offline conversion import and enhanced conversions for leads uploads migrate to the Data Manager API and are blocked in the Google Ads API; from April 2026 Google Ads accepts user-provided data from tags, Data Manager and API without choosing one method. Source: [Google Ads API docs, manage offline conversions](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/conversions/upload-offline); [Google Ads Help, about offline conversion imports](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2998031?hl=en)
- Tools exist that link WhatsApp workflows to Google Ads to send WhatsApp lead offline conversions automatically (vendor claim). Source: [WA Conversion Tracking](https://www.waconversiontracking.com/)
- Meta Conversions API for Business Messaging requires action_source: "business_messaging" and messaging_channel: "whatsapp"; using action_source "website" is the most common error. Sending purchase events lets Meta optimize delivery toward people likely to buy via messaging and measure ROAS on real purchases, with a default 7-day last-click attribution. Source: [Chatfuel](https://chatfuel.com/blog/whatsapp-conversions-api-ctwa-ads); [Insider Academy](https://academy.useinsider.com/docs/meta-conversions-api-for-click-to-whatsapp-ads)
- Consent Mode v2 is not explicitly mandated in Brazil (LGPD has its own consent rules), but it is used as an LGPD compliance tool; ANPD moved into active enforcement in 2024-2025. Source: [FlexyConsent](https://flexyconsent.com/blog/brazil-lgpd-anpd-cookie-consent-guide/); [Google Ads Help, LGPD](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9943919)
- From June 15, 2026, ad_storage in Consent Mode becomes the single parameter controlling what ad data reaches Google Ads (Google Signals no longer co-controls it); Analytics encrypts IPs before sending to linked Ads accounts. Source: [UniConsent](https://www.uniconsent.com/blog/google-ads-consent-mode-change-2026) (third-party summary; confirm on Google help)

### Inferences
- Current setup (conversion = WhatsApp click) trains Smart Bidding on intent, not revenue; bots, accidental taps and price-shoppers count the same as a booked R$200+ Ouro Preto transfer. Recommended: keep WhatsApp click as secondary conversion, make "booking confirmed" (uploaded offline with value) the primary, then test Maximize conversion value / tROAS once volume allows.
- Practical capture: prefill the WhatsApp message with a short reference code tied to the gclid stored in a cookie/sheet at click time (the site already uses ?rota= parameters, so this fits); dispatcher marks booking + fare; daily upload via Data Manager or a connector.
- Differentiate conversion value by route (airport to BH vs Ouro Preto/Inhotim) so bidding favors long transfers.

### Gaps
- No public benchmark on the share of WhatsApp clicks that become bookings for taxi/transfer in Brazil.
- Did not verify how Google counts message asset conversions ("message chat" conversion) in reporting; help page did not define it.

## 3. Meta Ads: click-to-WhatsApp, audiences, creatives

### Takeaway
Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) is the dominant Brazilian format; its conversations open a free 72-hour messaging window, and feeding bookings back through CAPI for Business Messaging is the main optimization lever. Costs rose in 2026 with tax pass-through.

### Cited Findings
- CTWA sends ad clicks directly into a WhatsApp conversation, removing landing pages/forms; described as the most efficient paid channel for Brazilian companies; typical Brazilian CPL R$3 to R$30 depending on sector (vendor estimate). Source: [Zenvia](https://zenvia.com/blog/como-usar-anuncios-click-to-whatsapp-no-seu-negocio/); [Message Central](https://www.messagecentral.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-brasil)
- Conversations started from CTWA ads or Facebook Page CTA open a 72-hour free entry point window from the business's first reply, in which all message types are free. Source: [Meta for Developers, WhatsApp pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)
- Meta began passing federal taxes (PIS/COFINS and ISS) to Brazilian advertisers in 2026, about 12.15% to 12.5% added to ad costs. Source: [Message Central](https://www.messagecentral.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-brasil) (secondary; confirm with Meta billing notice)
- Airport transfer marketing guides recommend segmenting business vs leisure travelers, corporate travel coordinators and event planners, and remarketing to people who compared providers. Source: [Sona](https://www.sona.com/blog/google-ads-for-airport-transportation-services-a-comprehensive-setup-guide)

### Inferences
- Audience ideas: custom audiences of site visitors by route page (Ouro Preto LP vs airport LP), people who messaged on WhatsApp but did not book (from CAPI events), past customers list for exclusion and for lookalikes; geo-target origin states of Confins flights with interest in Ouro Preto/Inhotim/Minas travel; Advantage+ audience with CAPI purchase optimization once enough booking events exist.
- Creative angles: filmed at the Confins credentialed taxi stand (proof of legitimacy vs clandestine drivers), fixed price vs app surge at night, driver waiting with name sign, UGC-style testimonials from tourists going to Ouro Preto/Inhotim.

### Gaps
- Meta removed most detailed "travel behavior" targeting options in recent years; could not confirm which traveler behaviors (e.g. "frequent travelers", "returned from travels") remain available in Brazil in 2026.
- No published CTWA cost per conversation benchmark specific to taxi/transfer.

## 4. Other channels: OTAs, partnerships, seasonality

### Takeaway
Viator/marketplaces work as paid acquisition priced on commission (roughly 20 to 30%), and Minas tourism peaks (Carnaval das cidades históricas, Inhotim's record visitation) justify event-based bursts.

### Cited Findings
- Viator lists airport and ground transfers as a category; local operators deliver while Viator handles payment, vouchers and support; supplier commission commonly cited as 20% to 30% (travel agents earn about 8%). Source: [MainStreet Travel Agency](https://mainstreetagencytravel.com/news/viator-travel-agent-how-it-works-commission-rates-amp-sign-up-guide); [Viator category page](https://www.viator.com/Europe-tours/Airport-and-Ground-Transfers/d6-g15-c52)
- Inhotim had a record 357,684 visitors in 2025 (projected 361k by year end, +8.5% vs 2024), celebrates 20 years in 2026, and was the only Brazilian destination in the NYT list of places to go in 2026. Source: [Agência Brasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/cultura/noticia/2025-12/museu-inhotim-em-minas-gerais-tem-recorde-de-visitacao-em-2025); [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/viagemegastronomia/viagem/inhotim-entra-para-lista-do-nyt-como-destino-para-conhecer-em-2026/)
- Carnaval 2026 in Minas historic cities drew over 376,000 people (+7.5% vs 2025), Ouro Preto was the most visited; Ouro Preto expected 60,000 tourists with 85% hotel occupancy. Source: [Agência Minas](https://www.agenciaminas.mg.gov.br/noticia/carnaval-em-minas-gerais-bate-recorde-de-publico-em-2026-com-14-9-milhoes-de-folioes-e-conquista-mais-turistas); [Jornal Voz Ativa](https://jornalvozativa.com/noticias/carnaval-de-ouro-preto-2026-com-85-de-ocupacao-cidade-espera-60-mil-turistas/)
- Ouro Preto hosts around 400 events per year. Source: [Mais Minas](https://maisminas.org/com-edital-da-unesco-e-400-eventos-por-ano-ouro-preto-vive-momento-historico-no-turismo-cultural/)

### Inferences
- Event calendar for bursts: Carnaval (Ouro Preto/Mariana), Festival de Inverno, Semana Santa in Ouro Preto, Inhotim peak months, big BH events/feiras at Expominas; pre-load budget 2 to 4 weeks ahead with "Presence or Interest" in origin cities.
- B2B/affiliate: pousadas in Ouro Preto and hotels near Inhotim/Pampulha can refer arriving guests for a fixed commission per ride; corporate accounts for companies in Vespasiano/Lagoa Santa/Confins region.

### Gaps
- Did not verify Viator/GetYourGuide current listings for Confins, nor Google Things to do / hotel partnership options for transfers.
- No data on corporate account programs used by Brazilian airport taxi cooperatives.
